WhatsApp Calendar Bot

A private, personal tool for one household. It is not offered to the public.

The bot is a member of a family WhatsApp group. When the people in the group agree on a meeting, it proposes the date and time in the group. Once someone confirms, it creates the event on the bot account's own Google Calendar and invites a fixed list of family members.

The bot runs on a personal computer. Its language model runs on the same machine, so chat messages are never sent to a third-party AI service.

Google access: the bot uses only the calendar.events permission, and only on the bot's own Google account. It cannot read anyone else's calendar, email or files.
